# Blow Up My Social Media

A Claude Code plugin for organic social growth on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube.

The name is the ambition, not a guarantee. Nothing here promises virality, followers, or sales. What it does is control the inputs: every piece is **built backward from retention** to earn **one** outcome (a follow, a comment, a share, a save, or a sale), on a route that works on the platform as it behaves today.

## What's inside

| Skill | Job |
|---|---|
| `blow-up-my-social-media` | The front door. Brand routing, the job line, the retention-backward build, platform files, quality gate. |
| `launch-pack` | One finished reel → native copy for all six platforms, first comments, and two ranked posting windows each. |
| `hand-raiser` | Demand-testing posts, the DM path from raised hand to paid yes, and a week built around one offer. |
| `comment-gate-loop` | Fulfils "comment WORD" promises: preflight, auto lane where allowed, manual checklist where not, a ledger so nobody is missed. |
| `platform-freshness-loop` | Re-verifies the platform files against dated sources, about monthly. |

Commands: `/blow-up`, `/series`, `/autopsy`. Every skill is also callable by name, for example `/launch-pack`.

## Why the platform files have dates

Platform advice rots. Each file in `references/platforms/` has a **Verified** date, a source list, and a tag on every claim: [P] primary source, [D] third-party original data, [I] inference. Anything older than 60 days gets re-checked before it shapes a deliverable.

## Bring your own brand kit

The plugin owns structure and platform mechanics. Voice comes from a brand kit skill you supply. With no kit, it works from voice samples you paste and tells you so; it never invents a brand voice.

## Not carried over from the skills this replaces

Deliberately left out, so nobody assumes it is here:

- Safe / Spicy / Savage post variants and an automatic 5 to 10 hook bank on every post. Default is one finished piece plus a runner-up hook; ask for variants when you want them.
- The 8 to 15 hashtag rule. Retired: hashtags are capped at 5 on Instagram and TikTok; the platform files say 0 to 3 on LinkedIn and 0 to 1 on X, and have no data for Facebook or YouTube.
- Any brand's tone guide, phrase bank, or hook library. Those belong in the brand kit.
- "Best mechanic" rankings. Nobody has published comparative data, so mechanics are listed, not ranked.
- TikTok reply-to-comment videos, sounds, duets, and stitches guidance, and posting-time defaults with no dataset behind them. Re-add only with a dated source.
- Handoffs to scoring tools or video-production skills. The plugin writes the script and the pack; producing or scoring the video is a separate tool's job.
