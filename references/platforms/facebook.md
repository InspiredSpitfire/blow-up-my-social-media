# Facebook

**Verified: 2026-09-20.** Tags: [P] primary source, [D] third-party original data or reporting, [I] inference or convention.

## How distribution works now

- **All video is Reels.** Since 2025-06 every video is shared as a Reel with no length or format restriction, and the Video tab is the Reels tab. [P1]
- **Link posts get a tiny share of views.** 98.9% of US Feed views in Q2 2026 went to posts with no external link. [P2] That figure also reflects how few link posts exist, so "link posts get low reach" is a likely inference, not a measured penalty. [I] Since 2025-12 Meta has tested capping Professional-mode profiles and Pages at 2 link posts a month unless they pay for Meta Verified; **links in comments are exempt.** [D3] Default: native post, link in the first comment.
- **Engagement bait is demoted.** Meta's Content Distribution Guidelines demote posts that explicitly request comments, tags, shares, or likes, and demote repeat offenders at the Page level. [P4] Reading "comment WORD for the link" as covered is an inference, and the safe one. [I] **Do not run "comment WORD" posts on Facebook.** Ask a real question; put the link in the first comment.
- **Originality.** Enforcement targets other people's content, low-value edits, stitched clips, and watermarks, with reduced distribution and loss of monetization; tightened again 2026-03-13. [D5][P6] Cross-posting your own Reels from Instagram is still supported. Any reach cost of cross-posting: unverified.

## Formats and cadence

- Reels for reach. A separate caption from Instagram, with the event or offer details in the text. [I]
- Conversational text and image posts that draw real comments outperform announcements. [I]
- Groups are active. For local and community brands, a group the brand owns is the relationship engine. [I]
- No cadence dataset was found for Facebook. Start at 3 to 5 Page posts a week and read the Page's own Insights. [I]

## Small-account reality

The unified Content Monetization program is still invite-only (register interest in the Professional Dashboard). [P7] Creator Fast Track (2026-03) is for creators with 100k+ followers elsewhere. [P8] Professional mode on profiles is live.

## Selling

Native checkout was phased out in 2025; product tags send buyers to your website. [D9] Routes: first-comment link, Events for workshops, Groups for warm audiences, Messenger replies done by hand.

## Local businesses

Before/after photos, project reveals with the location named, customer stories, and fast replies to comments and messages. [I]
