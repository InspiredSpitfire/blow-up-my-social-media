# TikTok (US)

**Verified: 2026-09-20.** Tags: [P] primary source, [D] third-party original data or reporting, [I] inference or convention. TikTok's support and API pages would not load for automated reads, so several rows are secondary. Re-verify before relying.

## The big change

TikTok US is run by **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC**, announced in a newsroom post dated 2026-01-23 (Oracle, Silver Lake, MGX at 15% each; ByteDance 19.9%). The joint venture retrains and updates the recommendation algorithm on US user data. Same app, same accounts; e-commerce and ads stay with TikTok's global entities. [P1] Creators reported reach swings after the change; nobody credible has quantified it. [D2] Don't quote a percentage. Claims that the retrained algorithm weights total watch time over completion have no TikTok source. [I]

## How distribution works

Every video gets tested on a small batch regardless of follower count; holding attention decides the next batch. That is long-standing creator consensus, not a dated TikTok statement. [I] Build for completion and rewatch, and read the retention graph before anything else.

## Formats

- **Length.** Uploads run to 60 minutes (secondary). [D3] The Creator Rewards Program pays only on original videos of **1 minute or more** and needs 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days; that is a payment rule, not proof longer video ranks better. [D3] The old 7-15 / 20-45 / 1-3 minute bands have no source. Pick the length the payoff needs and cut until the graph is flat.
- **Video vs photo carousels:** contested. Buffer finds video earns 77% more engagement; Metricool finds video gets 5.6x the views; another 2025 analysis favored carousels on engagement rate. [D4][D5] Default to video; test carousels for save-worthy reference content.
- **Hashtags:** capped at **5** since 2025-08. [D6] `#fyp` does nothing. [I]
- **Search.** Creator Search Insights shows content gaps, and since 2026-05 creators can add or remove the keywords assigned to a clip. [P7][D8] Say the phrase, show the phrase, caption the phrase.
- **Music.** Business accounts only get the Commercial Music Library. [P9]

## Cadence

2 to 5 posts a week is associated with +17% views per post and 11+ with +34%, but the median stays around 500 views at any cadence (11.4M posts). [D10] More posts buy more chances at an outlier, not a better typical post. Don't prescribe "1 to 3 a day".

## Comment-gates and DMs

ManyChat's TikTok **DM** automation went live in the US in 2026-01, Business accounts only. **Comment-triggered automation is not available in the US** ("pending approval"). [D11] Switching to a Business account also removes the general music library. [P9] Default: no comment-gate on TikTok; send people to the bio link or a keyword to DM, and fulfil by hand unless the user has set up and approved the DM automation.

## Bio link

Most 2026 sources say personal accounts need 1,000 followers and business accounts need none; one source says the threshold was dropped. No primary page. [D12] **Check the actual profile before writing "link in bio".**

## Selling

- TikTok Shop affiliate: 1,000 followers, and under 5,000 sits in a 30-day pilot (3 shoppable videos a day, 3 LIVEs a week). Official Shop Creators selling their own shop's goods have no follower minimum. [P13]
- **Digital products are prohibited** except the invite-only Virtual Goods category (courses, software, tickets; $500 max; no auto-renew). [P14] Fees are disputed between 6% and 8%. [D15]
- Practical route for a small account selling downloads or workshops: video → bio link (if eligible) → own page.
