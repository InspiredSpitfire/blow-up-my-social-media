# YouTube: long-form and Shorts

**Verified: 2026-09-20.** Tags: [P] primary source, [D] third-party original data or reporting, [I] inference or convention.

## How distribution works now

- Satisfaction signals (surveys, likes, dislikes) sit alongside click-through rate and watch time. Recommendations "pull" videos per viewer; judge a video over 90 days or more, not 48 hours. [D1]
- A video is a package: topic × title × thumbnail × first 30 seconds × structure. Most underperformance is packaging. [I]
- **View counts changed.** A Shorts view counts from the moment playback starts (since 2025-03-31); the old metric is now "engaged views". From 2026-08-24 long-form and live count the same way. [D2][D3] **Report engaged views, not raw views.**
- "Viewed vs swiped away" is a real Studio metric for Shorts. Calling it *the* key metric is creator consensus. [I]
- How small channels get tested: no 2025-26 statement found.

## Packaging

- **Test & Compare** now tests titles, thumbnails, or both, up to 3 variants, desktop only, advanced features required; the winner is picked by watch time per impression (since 2025-12-09). [D4] Ship every long-form video with three packages.
- Tags "play a minimal role". [P5] Titles under ~60 characters and "the first two description lines" are convention. [I]
- Decide title and thumbnail before filming. One idea per thumbnail; thumbnail text never repeats the title. [I]

## Long-form structure

Hook (promise or stakes, payoff shown if possible) → context and one open loop → 3 to 5 chapters that each re-hook and bridge → payoff no later than two-thirds in → one CTA. Built backward, like everything else.

## Shorts

- Up to **3 minutes** since 2024-10-15. [P6]
- Hook (visual and verbal together) → one idea → button that loops.
- **Links in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable.** The related-video link is. Channel profiles hold up to 14 clickable links. Long-form description links need advanced features on. [P7] Never write "link in the description" for a Short; point to the related video or the channel profile.

## Community

The Community tab is now "Posts". Communities is a separate, still-expanding feature; don't assume a channel has it. [D8]

## Small-channel reality

- **Hype** is live in the US but needs the Partner Program and 500 to 500,000 subscribers. [D9]
- Partner Program: from **2027-02-01** new applicants need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views, and Shorts ad revenue needs 10M Shorts views per 90 days; existing members keep their entry. [P10] An earlier-access tier at 500 subscribers (3 uploads plus 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views) unlocking fan funding and own-product Shopping was reported by the research pass but is **not in P10**: unverified, and whether it survives 2027-02-01 is unknown. Check YouTube Help before advising anyone on thresholds. [I]
- Inauthentic-content policy (2026-07-13): generic or template content, off-putting content, and AI personas giving advice on sensitive topics are out. AI-assisted content that adds original value stays monetizable. [D11]
- **AI disclosure** is required for realistic altered or synthetic content. Scripts, thumbnails, filters, clearly unrealistic content, and cloning your own voice are exempt. [P12]
- Cadence: the only large dataset excludes channels under 1,000 subscribers and is correlational. [D13] For a small channel, one good long-form video a week plus Shorts cut from it is a reasonable starting point. [I]

## Selling

Own merch through Shopping needs the Partner Program. [P14] Fourthwall is reported as a supported merch platform. [D15] The subscriber threshold for own-product Shopping is unverified; check the channel's Studio. No documented route for digital products: use the first description line and pinned comment on long-form, and the channel profile links for Shorts. Lead magnets that extend the video ("the checklist from this video") beat generic ones. [I]
