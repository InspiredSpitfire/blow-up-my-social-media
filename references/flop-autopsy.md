# Flop autopsy

Do not blame "the algorithm". Ask for the platform's own analytics first (retention graph, 3-second hold or viewed-vs-swiped, average view duration, read-through, impressions, CTR, sends, saves, comments, profile visits, link clicks, sales). If none are available, say so and diagnose from the content alone, labeled INFERENCE.

Work the path in order and stop at the **first** broken stage. Fixing a later stage while an earlier one is broken changes nothing.

| # | Symptom | Broken stage | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low impressions | Packaging or topic demand | Repackage (title, thumbnail, cover, first line). Do not remake the piece. On YouTube run Test & Compare. |
| 2 | Impressions fine, low hold or click | Hook | Rewrite the first 1 to 3 seconds or the first line as an honest promise of the payoff. |
| 3 | Hold fine, retention slides | Middle | Find the timestamp where the graph drops. Cut what sits there, move the payoff earlier, add a re-hook before it. |
| 4 | Watched, nothing happened | Ending | The piece had no single outcome. Rewrite the ending for one outcome (see retention-backward.md). |
| 5 | Engaged, no clicks or sales | Route or offer | Check the route works on this platform today (selling.md), the CTA is one action, and the page repeats the promise. |
| 6 | Clicked, didn't buy | Page or offer | Not a content problem. Fix the page or the offer. |

Platform-specific checks before concluding anything:

- **Policy demotion.** A "comment WORD" post on LinkedIn or Facebook, a link post on Facebook, or reposted or watermarked media on Instagram and Facebook can explain low reach by themselves.
- **YouTube:** judge over 90 days, and compare engaged views, not raw views (the counting changed).
- **TikTok US:** reach has been volatile since the 2026 algorithm retraining. One flop is not a pattern; compare against the account's last 10 posts.
- **X:** free accounts see a fraction of Premium impressions in third-party data; a mute or report costs far more than a like earns.
- **LinkedIn:** off-topic posts reach fewer out-of-network people because matching is by topic.

Output: the first broken stage, the evidence for it, the one change to make, and what to measure on the next post to confirm. One variable at a time.
