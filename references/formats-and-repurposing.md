# Formats and repurposing

Specs here defer to the platform files. If they disagree, the platform file wins.

## One idea, six native versions

Write the short-form version first; it forces clarity and is usually the hook of the long version. Never the same caption twice.

| Element | YouTube long | Shorts / Reels / TikTok | LinkedIn | X | Instagram carousel | Facebook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Promise + stakes, payoff shown | Visual and verbal hook together, first second | Line one is the hook, line two earns "see more" | The whole post is the hook | Cover slide is a hook, not a title | One conversational line |
| Length | What the payoff needs | What the payoff needs; Reels 3 min or less to be recommended | 1,301 to 2,500 characters tested best [D7 in linkedin.md] | One standalone post by default | 6 to 10 slides [I]; up to 20 allowed; 3:4 | Short, question-led |
| Built for [I] | Satisfaction and watch time | Hold and completion | Dwell and real comments | Replies and sends | Saves and sends | Real comments |
| CTA | Next video or pinned link | One action; route per platform file | A real question, "DM me WORD", or the link in the post | A reply prompt, or the link (test it) | Save, or comment-to-DM with fulfilment ready | A real question; link in first comment |
| Never | "Link in description" on a Short | Another app's watermark | "Comment WORD" gates | Bursts of posts; anything that earns a mute | More than 5 hashtags | "Comment WORD" asks; bare link posts |

## Carousel and document skeleton (Instagram, LinkedIn PDF, Facebook)

Ten slides is a default skeleton, not a rule [I]; cut slides before padding them. 1 Hook. 2 The problem in their words. 3 The turn (the insight). 4 to 8 one idea per slide, each opening with its own mini-promise. 9 The reframe. 10 The button line and one CTA. Built backward: write slide 10 first. Deliver visual direction per slide (what is on it, not just the words) and hand image generation to the brand's design skill with the kit's palette. Size for the 3:4 grid on Instagram.

## X thread, when one post won't hold it

Post one must stand alone and earn the reply by itself, because later posts decay in the same viewer's feed. Put the payoff in post one and the receipts after it.

## Trend scans

When asked what is working now: web search, 3 to 5 trend-aligned angles, each with where it came from and the date, and a version that fits the platform's rules and the brand's kit. Never promise a trend will carry a post.

## Tone profile (no kit yet)

Ask for 5 to 10 of the person's own posts. Describe vocabulary, rhythm, humor, sentence length, what they never say. Show the profile, get a yes, then offer to save it as the start of a brand kit. It is a description of how they already write, not an invention.

## Calendars

7, 30, or 90 days. Columns: date, platform, series and episode, job, hook, format, CTA, asset needed, status. Deliver as a table, and as a CSV file on request. Start from the cadence the person can sustain on a bad week.
